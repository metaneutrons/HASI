# Service-IT public research snapshot

Research date: 9 October 2026. Public-source summary prepared for publication
on 16 October 2026. The source is
[service.it.hs-hannover.de](https://service.it.hs-hannover.de/de/).

The anonymous linked-page inventory discovered 104 unique public HTML pages,
login redirects, aliases and a public RSS endpoint. This describes a crawl's
scope, not a complete content catalogue or an institutional access policy.

Raw crawl exports, authenticated captures and notes derived from authenticated
content remain local and are excluded from this repository. The public source
inventory utilities live in `docs/research/tools`; they are research utilities,
not a deployed knowledge connector.

The MVP still requires an approved connector/export, authoritative access
metadata, source citations and reliable change propagation. A single account's
ability to open a page does not authorize HASI to expose it to another user.
The source owner must settle those contracts before protected content is indexed
or returned to a model.
